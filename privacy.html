If you choose to connect a TikTok account, AI ClipForge uses TikTok's developer services to provide the requested integration. We request only the permissions needed for the features you choose to use. TikTok handles information according to its own policies and terms.

Information is used to provide requested features, generate clips and metadata, maintain and secure the service, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate requested features, maintain infrastructure, or comply with legal obligations.

You may choose whether to connect third-party accounts and whether to provide content for processing. You can stop using an integration by disconnecting it where the available controls permit.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the AI ClipForge operator through the contact method provided with the service.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be posted on this page.
